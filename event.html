Events

MAD Club events

  • Git Out'26 · 25-26 Even Sem · Escape Room + Hackathon
    A Git-themed escape room that unlocks a hackathon. Teams escaped with Git, cloned the problem statement they had unlocked, built a full-stack app from scratch, and pitched it.
  • Game Design'26 · 25-26 Even Sem · Talk + Game Dev Challenge
    An expert talk on how games are really made, then a build challenge: every participant drew a genre from a bowl and built a game of their own in it.
  • Appsylum'25 · 25-26 Odd Sem · Useless App Competition
    The Useless App Competition. Teams pitched gloriously impractical app ideas: ideation over code, humour over polish, in three fast phases.
  • Codathon · 24-25 Even Sem · Coding Marathon
    A coding marathon for building mobile features from scratch.
  • Pixel Pursuit'24 · 24-25 Odd Sem · Design & UI Sprint
    A pixel-themed UI design sprint.
  • Connect'24 · 23-24 Even Sem · Summit & Demo Day
    A showcase and networking day for the club's developers and designers.
  • TechSync'24 · 23-24 Even Sem · Multi-Club Arena
    A cross-club event with Visual Recall, Car Racing and Construction rounds.
  • InnoWave'24 · 23-24 Odd Sem · Hackathon & Pitch
    Two rounds, Trivia Battle and PitchPoint, built around mobile ideas and pitching them.
  • Mindspark'23 · 23-24 Odd Sem · Flagship Challenge
    The club's first flagship: three rounds, WikiWhiz, UI Clash and CodeMaze.

What we believe

Don't just use the future. Build it.
01 · Who we are

MAD Club (Mobile App Development Club) is a student-led club at New Horizon College of Engineering, Bengaluru. Started in 2023, it runs hackathons, build challenges and design events, and brings together students who want to make apps.

02 · Objective

Give students hands-on practice beyond the syllabus: building apps, working in teams, using real tools like Git, and pitching what they made.

03 · What members learn

Practical experience with industry tools (Git, Flutter, Unity, Figma), projects worth showing, and the confidence to pitch your work.

How the club runs

Committees

01 · Event Architecture & Execution

Program Committee

Designs competitive rounds, curates hackathon problem statements, coordinates technical mentors, and manages stage operations.

02 · Codebases & Technical Labs

Project Committee

Architects club internal tooling, conducts hands-on code labs in Flutter/Native stacks, and oversees member app development.

03 · Branding & Community Outreach

Marketing Committee

Crafts visual brand identity, motion teasers, Instagram campaigns, and runs the club's Instagram.

04 · Quality Assurance & Standards

Review Committee

Conducts code audits, evaluates hackathon submissions, assesses technical rubrics, and ensures flawless event delivery.
